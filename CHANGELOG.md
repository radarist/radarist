## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-14

First public prototype snapshot.

### Included

- local signal, entity, relationship, radar, graph, and report workflows;
- seeded Firebase-emulator and Neo4j demo data;
- human review of proposed relations and AI-assisted artifacts;
- local MCP and optional mission-runtime surfaces;
- generated capability inventory and reproducible public quality workflows.

### Boundaries

- local evaluation only; no hosted or production deployment claim;
- AI output is unverified until an operator reviews its evidence;
- provider-backed features require operator credentials and can incur cost;
- build missions and their sandbox are experimental, default-off, and not
  qualified or supported in v0.1.
